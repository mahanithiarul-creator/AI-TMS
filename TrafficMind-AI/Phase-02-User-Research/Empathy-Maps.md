# TrafficMind AI: Empathy Maps

**Research status:** Provisional synthesis from Phase 01. Validate with representative users across shifts, corridors, and accessibility needs.

## Asha Rao - Traffic Control Center Operator

| Thinks | Feels | Sees | Says | Does | Pain | Gain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| "Is this alert real and urgent?" | Responsible, time-pressured, cautious. | Camera feeds, alarms, queues, radio messages. | "Verify it before we change the plan." | Monitors, verifies, coordinates, logs. | Alert overload; partial visibility; downstream uncertainty. | Prioritized, explainable events and a clear response path. |
